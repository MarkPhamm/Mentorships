# Tips

Short, practical tips for networking and interviews. The [Mentality](../mentality/README.md) guide is how to think; these are things to do this week.

| | Tip | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Max out your LinkedIn connections](1-max-out-linkedin/README.md) | Use your weekly connection requests, connect with the people in the role you want, and aim for 1 to 2 coffee chats a week. |
| 2 | [Read the people, more than the role](2-read-the-people/README.md) | Prepare for the person, not only the job description. Early rounds are usually non-technical recruiters. |
| 3 | [Small talk wins](3-small-talk-wins/README.md) | People hire people they'd enjoy working with. Small talk breaks the ice and cuts minutes off the grilling. |
| 4 | [Coffee chat with the team before the interview](4-coffee-chat-the-team/README.md) | Talk to someone on the team before the interview: you learn the team and culture, and it shows initiative. |
| 5 | [Prepare, but don't over-prepare](5-dont-over-prepare/README.md) | Preparation has diminishing returns. Prepare your stories and fundamentals, then stop. |

— Mark Pham

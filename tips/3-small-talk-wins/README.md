# 3. Small talk wins

It's not just about how technical or how good you are. **It's also about how much people enjoy working with you.** Everyone in the final round can probably do the job. The one who gets the offer is often the one the team wants to sit next to.

## Why small talk matters

- **It breaks the ice.** The first minutes of an interview are tense for both sides. A real conversation relaxes you both, and you'll answer better once you're relaxed.
- **It shows how you'd be to work with.** Interviewers are quietly asking "would I enjoy a Monday standup with this person?" Small talk answers it before the first question.
- **It cuts 3 to 5 minutes off the interview.** A few minutes of good conversation is a few minutes less of grilling, and the questions that follow are friendlier.

## How to do it

- **Look them up, and find one genuine thing.** A shared school, a city, a post they wrote, a project their team shipped.
- **Ask about them.** "How long have you been on the team?" "What are you working on right now?" People enjoy talking about their work.
- **Be a person.** Mention something real about your week, a project you're excited about, a book. Keep it short and let them talk.
- **Read the room.** If they want to dive straight in, follow their lead. Small talk is a door, not a script.

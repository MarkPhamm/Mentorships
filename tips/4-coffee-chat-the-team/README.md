# 4. Coffee chat with the team before the interview

Once you have an interview scheduled, **find someone already on the team, or at the company, and ask for a 15-minute chat** before the interview.

## What it gets you

- **You understand the team.** What they work on, the stack, what a good first month looks like, what the manager cares about. Your answers stop being generic.
- **You understand the culture.** How they work, how they communicate, what they value. You'll know what to emphasise, and whether you actually want the job.
- **It shows initiative.** Most candidates don't do this. Saying *"I spoke with Linh on the analytics team last week, and she mentioned you're moving to dbt"* tells the interviewer you're the kind of person who does the extra step. (Illustration only.)
- **Sometimes it becomes a referral,** or a good word to the hiring manager.

## How to ask

Find them on LinkedIn and keep it short:

> *"Hi, I'm interviewing for the Analytics Engineer role on your team next week. Would you have 15 minutes to tell me a bit about how the team works? I'd really appreciate it."*

(An illustration, write your own.) Ask about their work, not about the interview questions. Thank them afterwards, and tell them how it went.

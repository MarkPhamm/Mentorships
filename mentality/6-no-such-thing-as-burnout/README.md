# 6. There's no such thing as burning out (unless you're a banker)

"Burnout" has become the most popular reason to slow down. Most of the time, it isn't burnout.

Run the test:

- **Do you sleep 8 hours a day?** You're not burning out.
- **Do you hit the gym 4 times a week?** You're not burning out.
- **Do you have time to scroll TikTok before you sleep?** You're not burning out.

If you have time to rest, exercise and kill an hour on your phone, you're not running on empty. You're tired, bored or unmotivated. Those are real, and they're normal, but they're not burnout. **Don't use the word as a reason to slow down.**

## What it actually looks like

Investment banking analysts are the classic example: 80- to 100-hour weeks, no weekends, sleeping a few hours a night for months, cancelling plans because a deck is due at 6 a.m. That's the kind of schedule that can genuinely break people.

If that's honestly you (you're not sleeping, not eating properly, can't function), then the answer is rest, and talking to someone you trust. That's not weakness. But be honest with yourself about which situation you're in. Most people saying "burnout" are nowhere near it.

## What to do instead of slowing down

- **Tired?** Sleep more and scroll less. Don't work less.
- **Bored?** You're probably lifting a weight you've outgrown. [Push harder](../5-push-to-failure/README.md), not softer.
- **Unmotivated?** Motivation follows action, not the other way around. Start the smallest next step and the rest comes.
- **Overwhelmed?** You need a plan, not a break. Write down everything, pick the one thing that matters most, and do that.

The people who get ahead aren't the ones who never feel tired. They're the ones who don't let feeling tired become a reason to stop.

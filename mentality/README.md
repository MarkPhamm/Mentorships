# Mentality

The other guides are about **what** to put on your LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio and resume. This one is about **how to think** while you job hunt. In my experience, the mindset decides more outcomes than the formatting does.

Four ideas, each in its own page. Read them in order; they build on each other.

| | Idea | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Don't be a minion](1-dont-be-a-minion/README.md) | If you do what everyone else does, you will not get hired. |
| 2 | [Play it like poker](2-play-it-like-poker/README.md) | Say whatever you want, as long as you can back it up. Don't bluff too little or too much. |
| 3 | [Detach](3-detachment/README.md) | The more desperate you are, the further you are from success. |
| 4 | [Find your competitive advantage](4-competitive-advantage/README.md) | Why would I hire you instead of an Ivy League kid? Find what's only true about you, then pitch it everywhere. |

## How they fit together

- **A minion** blends in because they're afraid of standing out.
- **A desperate player** bluffs badly: they either fold too early or go all-in on a hand they can't back up.
- **A detached player** can afford to be different and to play their hand with confidence, because no single outcome will break them.
- **Your competitive advantage** is the hand you're playing. Without one, there's nothing to bet on.

Find your advantage, and you have something worth showing. Don't be a minion, and people notice it. Play it like poker, and you get through the door. Detachment keeps you steady enough to do all of it.
